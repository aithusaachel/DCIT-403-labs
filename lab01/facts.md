- The Computer Science Department office is in the CS Department building, Room CS10. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 4:00pm, and closed on weekends and public holidays.
- The Departmental Registration Desk is in Room CS10 and handles course registration problems, Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 3:00pm.
- Balme Library is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00am to 10:00pm, and Sunday, 2:00pm to 8:00pm.
- During the examination period, Balme Library is open until midnight every day.
- Course registration is done online on the student portal. Students must pay at least 60% of their fees before they can register.
- The late registration fee is GHS 200.
- The campus shuttle on the Main Gate - Great Hall route runs on weekdays from 06:30, every 30 minutes until 21:00, and on Saturdays every hour from 08:00 to 18:00.
- The Akuafo - Science shuttle stops at the CS Department on weekdays from 07:15, every 20 minutes until 19:15. There is no Saturday service.
- The Pent - Commonwealth shuttle runs on weekdays from 06:45, every 15 minutes until 20:00.
- A lost student ID card is replaced at the Academic Affairs office. The replacement fee is GHS 50.
- The University Hospital at Legon treats students. For emergencies, call the National Ambulance Service on 112.
- Hall room change requests are handled by the hall administrator in Week 3 of the semester only.
- The CS computer labs are open to students Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 8:00pm.
- The Night Market is near the Main Gate shuttle stop and is open every evening from 5:00pm.
